# Ghost Partner Network Plan

Client: Haven Design & Build LLC  
Goal: Build local referral authority, citations, backlinks, and trusted partner visibility.

## Priority Partner Types

- Realtors serving Marietta, East Cobb, Smyrna, Roswell, Kennesaw, and greater Atlanta.
- Interior designers and decorators.
- Architects and plan designers.
- Flooring, cabinet, tile, lighting, countertop, deck material, and fixture suppliers.
- Commercial property managers.
- Local business associations and chambers.
- Insurance/restoration-adjacent partners when project fit is appropriate.

## Monthly Outreach Targets

- 5 supplier/vendor link or partner-listing requests.
- 5 realtor/designer/architect referral introductions.
- 2 local publication or community feature pitches.
- 2 chamber/directory/citation updates.

## Outreach Angle

Haven Design & Build LLC is a Marietta-area residential and light commercial renovation partner for kitchens, bathrooms, basements, decks, outdoor living, garages, office updates, retail improvements, and tenant build-outs.

## Tracking

Use `visibility/CITATION_TRACKER.csv` and weekly growth reports to record:

- Contact name.
- Organization.
- URL.
- Outreach date.
- Follow-up date.
- Result.
- Backlink/citation status.
- Referral potential.
