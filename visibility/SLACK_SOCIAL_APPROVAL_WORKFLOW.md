# Slack Social Approval Workflow

Client: Haven Design & Build LLC  
Slack workspace: Design Haven Build  
Purpose: Social, Google Business Profile, and growth-content approvals

## Recommended Channels

- `#all-design-haven-build`: weekly growth summaries, KPI updates, approvals needed, final posted links.
- `#social`: social/GBP post drafts, asset requests, approval threads, captions, images, hashtags.
- `#new-channel`: temporary/general channel; archive or repurpose after approval workflow is established.

## Approval Rules

Use emoji reactions in `#social`:

- `:white_check_mark:` Approved to publish.
- `:eyes:` Needs review from Micah.
- `:pencil2:` Needs copy change.
- `:framed_picture:` Needs photo/visual.
- `:x:` Do not publish.

Approval source of truth:

- A post is publish-ready only when Micah or the assigned approver gives `:white_check_mark:` or writes `approved`.
- If no approval is visible, keep the post in draft status.
- If asset/photo approval is unclear, ask before posting.

## Weekly Flow

Monday:

- GEO prepares weekly visibility and competitor notes.
- Vega prepares one social post and one Google Business Profile post.
- Post both drafts in `#social`.
- Add required asset/request notes.

Tuesday:

- Micah reviews drafts.
- Ghost updates copy or swaps photos if needed.

Wednesday:

- Approved social post is published or scheduled.
- Approved GBP post is published or scheduled.

Friday:

- Drop posted links and quick performance notes in `#all-design-haven-build`.
