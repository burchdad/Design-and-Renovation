Composite Deck Stairs & Landing

New composite stairs and a landing gave this outdoor space a cleaner, more functional connection between the home, deck, and yard. The finished details were selected to look polished in the daylight and work safely after dark.

Plan an Outdoor Project

Completed Features

  • Composite decking for a durable outdoor surface
  • Hidden fasteners for a cleaner board surface
  • Custom metal railing
  • Picture framing in contrasting colors
  • Integrated step lighting

Design Intent

The project combines practical circulation with more finished architectural details. Contrasting picture framing defines the deck edges, while the railing and lighting keep the stairs usable and visually connected to the property.

Why the Details Matter

Stairs and landings carry a lot of daily use. A well-planned layout, durable decking, clean fastener treatment, secure railing, and lighting can make an outdoor route feel more intentional, easier to use, and ready for entertaining.
