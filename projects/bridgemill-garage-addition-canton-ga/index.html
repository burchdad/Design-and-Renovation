Garage Addition & Exterior Renovation

BridgeMill Garage Addition in Canton, GA

This garage addition was designed to feel connected to the existing home while bringing a more modern, finished look to the property. The result combines exterior continuity, clean lighting details, and a durable garage interior.

Discuss Your Addition

Project Location

BridgeMill, Canton, Georgia. The public case study identifies the community and city only; no private street address is published.

Project Goal

Create a garage addition that looks like it had always been part of the home, while updating the exterior and interior with a cohesive modern finish.

Exterior Details

  • Hardie siding with black soffits, trim, and accents
  • Custom color-matched gutters and downspouts
  • Metal and glass garage doors
  • Gimbal spotlights, recessed lighting, and linear accent lighting
  • Exterior details coordinated with the existing home

Garage Interior Details

  • Side-mounted garage door openers to keep ceilings clean
  • Epoxy garage flooring
  • Recessed can lights and hex lighting
  • Finished shiplap walls, ceilings, and trim
  • Functional, finished space for vehicles and storage

What Makes an Addition Feel Original

Matching the larger design language matters: siding, trim, gutters, roofline details, door design, lighting placement, and finish transitions all work together. This project focused on those small details so the new garage feels integrated rather than appended.
