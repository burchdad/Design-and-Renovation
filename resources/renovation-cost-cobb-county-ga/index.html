What Affects Renovation Cost in Cobb County, GA?

Renovation pricing depends on the project scope, existing conditions, finish selections, and how much coordination is needed. Haven Design & Build reviews the details before giving responsible next steps.

Request a Project Fit Review

Scope Drives Cost

A cosmetic refresh is different from a layout change, structural adjustment, full kitchen remodel, bathroom renovation, basement finish, deck build, or commercial improvement.

Materials Matter

Cabinetry, tile, counters, fixtures, flooring, waterproofing, lighting, appliances, and exterior materials can all shift the final project range.

Site Conditions Matter

Existing plumbing, electrical access, moisture conditions, framing, permits, and demolition complexity can change what the project requires.
