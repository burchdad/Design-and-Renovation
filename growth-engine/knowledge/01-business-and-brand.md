## Positioning

Haven is a design-build remodeling contractor focused on thoughtful residential improvements and select light-commercial renovations. The website’s established brand voice is premium, straightforward, and detail-oriented: dark black and gold visuals; confident but not flashy language; visible craftsmanship; practical spaces that hold up.

Preferred themes:

- Built around the way clients live or work.
- Details that are intentional and durable.
- Clear process and considered result.
- Useful improvements over vague luxury claims.

Avoid:

- Unsupported “best,” “#1,” “award-winning,” “luxury,” or “guaranteed” claims.
- Excessively fine or filler-heavy copy.
- Overly casual social-media language on core website pages.
- Invented credentials, coverage areas, reviews, budgets, or project outcomes.
